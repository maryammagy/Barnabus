# Twenty-Hour Scope Plan

## Planning rule

The 20-hour cap includes Stage 1 work. Actual time replaces planned time as the work proceeds; the time log is authoritative. Work stops when the cap is reached. The order is fixed: secure all hard gates, complete every required deliverable at a concise minimum viable standard, deepen the highest-weight scored work, and attempt no more than one ceiling item only if time remains.

## Deliberate omissions unless required for a gate

- Do not attempt the other six ceiling items.
- Do not maximize page counts; concise completeness beats volume.
- Do not build broad exploratory notebooks or presentation polish before gate evidence exists.
- Do not reproduce/decompose the analyst headline as a ceiling exercise unless it becomes essential to a hard gate; the supplied SQL remains unexecuted until a later authorized stage.
- Do not resolve ambiguous artifact intent by inventing files, answers, or authorization.

## Reallocation rule

Time saved inside an earlier block remains in that priority tier until its exit condition is independently verified. A failed or uncertain hard gate consumes all downstream contingency, including the ceiling hour. Page-limit editing cannot weaken memo/presentation claim consistency or omit required disclosures.
